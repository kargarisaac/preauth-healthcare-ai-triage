### Initial Outreach Angles (by persona)

- Claims/Operations: measurable turnaround reduction, denial rate impact, FTE hours saved; 6–8 week pilot with success criteria.
- CMO/Medical: safety guardrails, rule transparency, concordance metrics, human-in-loop; retrospective backtest on past cases.
- IT/Security: eClaimLink/Shafafiya XML fit, hosting in UAE, audit logging, access controls, minimal integration path.
- CEO/CFO/BD: ROI summary, market differentiation, regulator-aligned compliance story.

Proven KPIs to propose: <6 seconds average decision latency; <$0.10 cost per case; targeted auto-approve rate with high concordance to human decisions.
