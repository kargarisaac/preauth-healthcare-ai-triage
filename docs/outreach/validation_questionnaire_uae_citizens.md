## Validation Questionnaire — UAE Citizens’ Insurance Experience

Purpose: Qualitative insights on prior authorization and claims experience. Define unfamiliar terms in brackets on first use during interviews.

Screeners
- Are you currently covered by UAE health insurance? (Yes/No)
- Have you or a family member needed prior authorization [pre-approval from insurer before treatment] in the last 12 months? (Yes/No)

Core Questions (15–20 mins)
1) How do you usually access care? Public/private? Which insurer/TPA handles your plan?
2) Have you experienced delays related to prior authorization? Describe the steps and timelines.
3) What communication did you receive (app, SMS, calls)? Was status tracking clear?
4) If a request was denied initially, what happened next? How long did it take to resolve?
5) How satisfied were you with the process (1–10)? What would improve it most?
6) Did your provider help navigate the process? What worked/what didn’t?
7) Data privacy: do you have concerns about where your health data is stored/processed?
8) If approvals were near-instant, how would that change your experience?

Wrap-Up
- Permission to follow-up? May we anonymize your inputs for product decisions?
