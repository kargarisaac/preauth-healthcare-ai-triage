Nazmito – AI-Enriched Pre-Authorization for Chronic Care

The Pain

Pre-authorization in the UAE runs on solid digital rails (Shafafiya/eClaimLink), but logic is still static and manual. Chronic patients (diabetes, HTN, CKD) generate preventable costs because gaps aren’t closed proactively. Manual reviews eat time and money (≈40% touched by humans, ~3-day turnaround), and providers are frustrated.

What Nazmito Does

We transform UAE healthcare data into intelligent clinical decisions through a modern AI architecture:

  • Multi-format ingestion: XML (eClaimLink/Shafafiya), CSV, PDFs, scanned images with OCR
  • FHIR-compliant normalization: Canonical schema with UAE extensions, medallion data architecture
  • AI-powered reasoning: Knowledge graphs (KuzuDB), vector embeddings, LLM agents (LangGraph)
  • Smart authorization: Enriched pre-approval pathways with cost optimization and guideline adherence
  • Full explainability: Audit trails, model versioning, clinical citations, compliance reporting

Why UAE / Why Now

Mandatory coverage expands to ~9.3M lives. Compliance (PDPL/ADHICS) and local data localization create a moat. TPAs/insurers already digitized, but not “intelligentized.” Regulators push for faster PA decisions; payers need MLR relief on chronic members.
