Process

How Nazmito Works

1
🔍

Nazmito Ingest™: See the Full Member Picture

Our engine ingests claims, labs, and pharmacy data. We normalize formats, score data quality, and compare history against clinical guidelines to spot care gaps instantly.

2
🎯

Nazmito Decide™: Shift From Approval to Action

Instead of a simple yes/no, our rules and ML models craft an "enriched, pre-approved pathway." If a diabetes check is overdue, we bundle it. If a lower-cost drug equivalent exists, we suggest it and reward providers who adopt it.

3
📊

Nazmito Explain™: Trust Through Transparency

Every decision comes with a complete audit trail: which rules fired, which model version was used, and the clinical citation. This explainability builds trust and simplifies compliance.

4
🧠

Nazmito Intelligence™: Create Localized Intelligence

Outcome signals feed reinforcement-learning loops that personalize and localize our models to UAE member populations, ensuring the system gets smarter and more effective every week.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you replace our current TPA portal?

+

No. We sit alongside or integrate into it. Think "intelligence layer," not "portal replacement."

How long to integrate?

+

A lightweight API or SFTP batch can go live in 4–6 weeks. We've budgeted an "integration sprint" in every pilot.

Who owns the decision?

+

You do. Nazmito is decision support. We provide rationale and audit trails; you keep the final call.

How do you prove savings?

+

We agree on KPIs up front (manual rate, turnaround, proxy MLR markers). We run a baseline, then show the delta after deployment.
