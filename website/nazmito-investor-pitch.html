Competition

The rails exist (Shafafiya, eClaimLink). TPAs and insurers run basic rule engines. InterQual/Milliman provide static criteria. Global AI giants focus on US markets. No one in the UAE delivers proactive, AI-driven, chronic-care-oriented authorization with transparent ROI.

  • Status quo: NAS, Neuron, NextCare, MedNet portals – rule checks, manual queues.
  • Guideline vendors: InterQual / MCG – evidence rules, little personalization.
  • Global tech: Optum, eviCore – strong tech, weak local compliance/footprint.

Nazmito wins on UAE-first compliance, incentive bundling, explainable AI, and chronic outcome focus.
