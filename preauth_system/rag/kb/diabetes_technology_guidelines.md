# Diabetes Technology Guidelines

## Coverage Criteria for Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM)

### Medical Necessity Requirements

Pre-authorization for Continuous Glucose Monitoring devices requires documentation of ALL the following criteria:

1. **Confirmed Diabetes Diagnosis**
   - Type 1 diabetes mellitus (E10.x) OR Type 2 diabetes mellitus (E11.x) with insulin dependency
   - Diagnosis documented by endocrinologist or diabetes specialist
   - Laboratory confirmation with HbA1c ≥ 6.5% or fasting glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL [1]

2. **Insulin Therapy Requirements**
   - Current treatment with multiple daily insulin injections (≥3 injections/day) OR
   - Continuous subcutaneous insulin infusion (insulin pump therapy)
   - Documentation of insulin regimen for minimum 3 months prior to CGM request [2]

3. **Glycemic Control Documentation**
   - HbA1c ≥ 7.0% (53 mmol/mol) despite intensive insulin management OR
   - History of severe hypoglycemic episodes (≥2 episodes requiring assistance in past 6 months) OR
   - Documented hypoglycemia unawareness with Clarke or Gold score ≥ 4 [3]

4. **Frequency of Blood Glucose Monitoring**
   - Self-monitoring blood glucose ≥ 4 times daily for minimum 30 days prior to request
   - Documentation of glucose log or meter downloads demonstrating compliance
   - Evidence of appropriate response to glucose readings with insulin adjustments [4]

### Clinical Documentation Requirements

#### Physician Assessment
- Comprehensive diabetes evaluation by endocrinologist or certified diabetes educator
- Assessment of patient motivation and ability to use CGM technology appropriately
- Documentation that conventional glucose monitoring is insufficient for optimal care
- Plan for CGM data interpretation and diabetes management adjustments [5]

#### Patient Education Requirements  
- Completion of structured diabetes self-management education program
- Demonstration of competency in:
  - Carbohydrate counting and insulin-to-carb ratios
  - Correction factor calculations  
  - Recognition and treatment of hypoglycemia
  - Proper CGM sensor insertion and calibration procedures [6]

### Exclusions and Contraindications

CGM coverage is NOT approved for:
- Type 2 diabetes managed with diet and oral medications only
- Gestational diabetes without pre-existing diabetes
- Patients unable to perform self-monitoring blood glucose ≥ 2 times daily
- Active psychiatric conditions preventing safe device use
- Pregnancy (requires specialized maternal-fetal medicine evaluation) [7]

## Coverage Criteria for Insulin Pump Therapy

### Fundamental Requirements

Pre-authorization for Continuous Subcutaneous Insulin Infusion (CSII) requires documentation of ALL the following criteria:

1. **Diabetes Type and Duration**
   - Type 1 diabetes mellitus diagnosed ≥ 6 months prior to request OR
   - Type 2 diabetes with documented C-peptide deficiency (< 0.6 ng/mL) 
   - Failed intensive insulin regimen (multiple daily injections) for minimum 6 months [8]

2. **Glycemic Control Challenges**  
   - HbA1c ≥ 7.5% (58 mmol/mol) despite optimal multiple daily insulin therapy OR
   - Recurrent severe hypoglycemia (≥ 2 episodes requiring assistance annually) OR
   - Wide glycemic variability with coefficient of variation ≥ 36% [9]

### Advanced Indication Criteria

#### Dawn Phenomenon Management
- Documented dawn phenomenon requiring pre-dawn insulin administration
- Inability to achieve target morning glucose (80-130 mg/dL) with conventional therapy
- Evidence of attempted solutions including bedtime insulin timing adjustments [10]

#### Lifestyle and Occupational Requirements
- Unpredictable meal timing due to work/lifestyle demands  
- Variable physical activity requiring frequent insulin adjustments
- Travel requirements across multiple time zones affecting insulin timing
- Professional requirements for precise glycemic control (pilots, professional drivers) [11]

### Pre-Pump Education and Assessment

#### Mandatory Education Components
- Carbohydrate counting certification with demonstrated accuracy ≥ 85%
- Insulin sensitivity factor determination and validation
- Correction bolus calculations with supervised practice
- Sick day management protocols and ketone monitoring
- Pump malfunction recognition and emergency procedures [12]

#### Psychological Assessment
- Evaluation by certified diabetes educator or psychologist
- Assessment of:
  - Motivation for intensive diabetes management
  - Understanding of pump therapy risks and benefits  
  - Ability to perform complex problem-solving
  - Support system adequacy for pump therapy success [13]

### Pump Technology Selection Criteria

#### Standard Insulin Pump Features
- Programmable basal rates (minimum 24 different rates/day)
- Multiple bolus delivery options (normal, square wave, dual wave)
- Integrated bolus calculator with customizable parameters
- Comprehensive alarm system for occlusion, low battery, empty reservoir
- Waterproof rating IPX8 or equivalent for UAE climate conditions [14]

#### Advanced Feature Considerations
- Integrated continuous glucose monitoring capability
- Predictive low glucose suspend functionality
- Automated insulin delivery (hybrid closed-loop) capability
- Smartphone connectivity for remote monitoring (pediatric patients)
- Integration with UAE healthcare electronic medical records [15]

## Monitoring and Follow-up Requirements

### Initial Assessment Period
- Clinic visits at 1 week, 1 month, and 3 months post-initiation
- HbA1c measurement at 3-month intervals
- Download and review of device data at each visit
- Assessment of hypoglycemia frequency and severity
- Evaluation of patient satisfaction and quality of life metrics [16]

### Ongoing Coverage Criteria
- Demonstrated improvement in glycemic control OR reduction in hypoglycemia
- Compliance with recommended clinic follow-up schedule
- Appropriate use of device features and data interpretation
- Absence of repeated severe hypoglycemia or DKA episodes
- Annual diabetes education refresher training completion [17]

### Coverage Renewal Requirements
Annual renewal requires documentation of:
- Continued medical necessity based on original criteria
- HbA1c within individualized target range OR documented improvement ≥ 0.5%
- Maintenance of diabetes self-care skills and device competency
- Cost-effectiveness analysis compared to conventional therapy
- Patient-reported outcome measures demonstrating benefit [18]

## Cost-Effectiveness Considerations

### Economic Evaluation Criteria
- Total cost of diabetes technology vs. conventional therapy over 2-year period
- Reduction in acute diabetes complications (ER visits, hospitalizations)
- Improvement in work productivity and quality-adjusted life years
- Long-term cost savings from improved glycemic control
- Integration with UAE national diabetes prevention strategy [19]

### Budget Impact Assessment
- Annual healthcare system cost analysis
- Allocation within diabetes care budget priorities
- Comparison with international diabetes technology adoption rates
- Population health impact modeling for UAE diabetes prevalence
- Sustainability analysis for expanding coverage criteria [20]
