The Problem

Pre‑authorization is supposed to protect payers from unnecessary spend. In reality, it became a paperwork bottleneck. Providers upload documents, reviewers scan them, and everyone waits. The UAE already has digital rails—Shafafiya in Abu Dhabi, eClaimLink in Dubai—but the logic running on those rails is still basic. Chronic patients drift through without proactive guidance, and avoidable complications eat budgets.

How Nazmito Works

1. Ingest & Enrich

See the Full Member Picture

We pull claims, labs, and pharmacy data (and EMR when available). We normalise formats, score data quality, and compare against clinical guidelines to spot gaps.

2. Decide & Incentivise

Approve Smartly, Not Just Quickly

Rules and ML models craft an “enriched, pre‑approved pathway.” If a diabetes check is overdue, we bundle it. If a cheaper but equivalent drug exists, we suggest it and reward providers who adopt it.

3. Explain & Learn

Transparent Decisions, Continuous Learning

Every approval carries an audit trail: which rules fired, which model version, and the clinical citation. Outcomes loop back to improve the engine and prove ROI to you.

FAQ

Do you replace our current TPA portal?

No. We sit alongside or integrate into it. Think “intelligence layer,” not “portal replacement.”

How long to integrate?

A lightweight API or SFTP batch can go live in 4–6 weeks. We’ve budgeted an "integration sprint" in every pilot.

You do. Nazmito is decision support. We provide rationale and audit trails; you keep the final call.

How do you prove savings?

We agree on KPIs up front (manual rate, turnaround, proxy MLR markers). We run a baseline, then show the delta after deployment.
